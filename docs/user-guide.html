1. User Roles & Access Levels

1.1 Role Overview

The system enforces four (4) distinct roles. Every staff account is assigned exactly one role by the Administrator. A user cannot elevate their own role.

RoleDescriptionTypical Assigned To
Admin Full system access. Can manage users, delete records, run backups, configure tax rates, and view all reports and audit logs. Municipal Treasurer, IT Administrator
Cashier Can post payments, generate official receipts, and view the payment ledger and reports. Cannot add or edit property records. Revenue Collection Clerk, Cashier
Encoder Can add and edit property records and post payments. Cannot manage users or run system maintenance tasks. Assessment Clerk, Data Encoder
Viewer Read-only access. Can search properties, view payment history, and view reports. Cannot make any changes. Auditor, Mayor's Office Staff, Supervisor

2. System Modules

2.1 Dashboard

All Roles

The Dashboard is the first screen displayed after login. It provides a real-time financial snapshot of the municipality's revenue collection status.

What you will see:

  • Total Collection — cumulative revenue collected to date, this month, and today
  • Total Properties — number of active registered properties in the system
  • Recent Payments — the last 8 payments posted, with OR number, property, and amount
  • Collection Trend Chart — monthly collection over the past 6 months
  • Revenue by Barangay — visual breakdown of collections per barangay
Tip: The Dashboard figures refresh automatically. If you just posted a payment and the totals have not updated, wait a few seconds and refresh the page.

2.2 Property Records

Encoder Admin (View only for Cashier and Viewer)

The master registry of all real properties subject to Real Property Tax (RPT) within the municipality. Every taxable property must be registered here before any payment can be posted against it.

Key functions:

  • Add New Property — enter the Tax Declaration Number (TDN), owner name, assessed value, location, barangay, kind of property (Residential, Commercial, Agricultural, Industrial, Special), and effectivity date
  • Edit Property — update assessed value when a new assessment is issued by the Assessor's Office, or update owner name upon transfer of ownership
  • Search — find a property by TDN, PIN, owner name, or location. The system supports fuzzy name matching — typing "dela crus" will still find "DELA CRUZ"
  • Property Dossier — view the complete history of a property: all payments posted, all assessment changes, and the full audit trail
  • Soft Delete — remove a property from active records without permanently deleting it. Deleted properties can be restored by an Administrator
  • Bulk Import — upload a formatted Excel file to add or update hundreds of properties at once (Admin only). The system validates the file before committing
  • Bulk Barangay Update — reassign multiple properties to a different barangay in one operation
Important: The Assessed Value entered here is the basis for all tax computations. Ensure it matches the current Tax Declaration issued by the Municipal Assessor's Office before saving.

2.3 Payment & Receipt Ledger

Cashier Encoder Admin

The primary working screen for revenue collection staff. All tax payments are posted here and official receipts are generated from this module.

Key functions:

  • Post a Payment — search for the property by TDN or owner name, then enter: OR number, date paid, amount, tax year(s) covered, and penalty or discount if applicable. The system automatically allocates the payment across billing years, oldest year first.
  • Generate Official Receipt (PDF) — after posting, generate the OR receipt PDF. The system assigns the next sequential OR number automatically using a locked counter — no two cashiers can ever receive the same OR number simultaneously.
  • View Payment History — see all payments for a property with OR numbers, dates, tax years covered, and amounts
  • Reprint Receipt — regenerate the PDF for any previously posted payment
  • Delete a Payment — void a wrongly posted payment. The system automatically reverses the billing balance for the affected tax year(s).
The OR Number is a legal document identifier. It is sequential, unique, and enforced at the database level. Once issued, it cannot be reused or reassigned. Treat it with the same care as a physical official receipt booklet.

2.4 Delinquency Dashboard

All Roles

Displays all properties with outstanding RPT balances — properties where the tax due for one or more years has not been fully paid. This is the primary tool for the collection enforcement workflow.

What you will see:

  • Property TDN, owner name, and location
  • Total amount due (all unpaid years combined)
  • Total amount paid to date
  • Outstanding balance
  • Filter by barangay to focus collection efforts geographically

Documents you can generate from here:

  • Delinquency Computation Sheet (PDF) — the formal document showing the full breakdown of how the balance was computed, including the annual tax, penalty per year, and running total. Required for formal demand letters.
  • Notice of Delinquency (PDF) — the official notice to be served to the property owner, as required under Section 254 of RA 7160 (Local Government Code).
  • Statement of Account (PDF) — a summary statement showing all billing years, amounts due, amounts paid, and the current balance.

2.7 Assessment Roll

Encoder Admin Viewer

A paginated list of all active properties with their current assessed values — the official Assessment Roll as required by the Department of Finance and DILG. Used to verify that all properties are properly assessed before the start of each tax year.

  • Displays TDN, owner name, location, kind of property, and assessed value
  • Can be exported to Excel for submission to the Provincial Assessor's Office
  • Supports filtering by kind of property and barangay

2.8 Audit Trail

Admin

Every action performed in the system is permanently recorded in the Audit Trail — who did what, when, on which record, and what the data looked like before and after the change. This log is immutable: no user, including the Administrator, can edit or delete any audit record.

Search and filter by:

  • Username — see the complete activity history of a specific staff member
  • Date range — see all actions performed on a given day or period
  • Action type — filter for CREATE, UPDATE, DELETE, RESET_PASSWORD, SOFT_DELETE, RESTORE, BULK_UPDATE, and more
COA Compliance: The Audit Trail is your primary evidence document for Commission on Audit (COA) examinations. If an auditor asks "who changed this assessed value and when," this is where you look. Records are retained for a minimum of 10 years per COA Circular 2009-006.

2.9 System Health

Admin

Monitors the technical health of the system in real time. This screen is for the Administrator and IT staff — not for daily revenue operations.

What is monitored:

  • Database — connection status and pool usage
  • Background Workers — status of PDF generation and backup job workers (healthy / stale / dead)
  • Storage — available disk space on the backup volume
  • Last Backup — timestamp, verification result, and checksum of the most recent backup
  • Cache — Redis connection status (if configured)
  • Vault — secrets manager accessibility

If PDF receipts are not generating or backups are failing, check this screen first. The worker status will indicate whether a job is stuck or a thread has died.

Sub-sectionPurpose

Tax PolicyConfigure the Basic Rate, SEF Rate, and Penalty Rate per tax year. Default is 1% + 1% = 2% total annual tax, 2% monthly penalty.

3. Delinquency Computation

3.1 Legal Basis

Real Property Tax (RPT) delinquency computation in this system follows Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991), specifically:

  • Section 246 — RPT is due on January 1 of each year and may be paid in four equal installments
  • Section 255 — A penalty of two percent (2%) per month is imposed on unpaid tax from the date it became due until fully paid, not to exceed thirty-six (36) months
  • Section 276 — Discount of up to 20% may be granted for advance or prompt payment (as determined by local ordinance)

3.2 The Basic Formula

For each unpaid tax year, the system computes the following:

Annual Tax Due = Assessed Value × (Basic Rate + SEF Rate) = Assessed Value × 0.02 (at default 1% + 1%) Penalty = Annual Tax Due × Penalty Rate × Months Late = Annual Tax Due × 0.02 × number of months overdue Discount = Amount granted for early/prompt payment (if applicable) Total Due = Annual Tax Due + Penalty − Discount

The penalty clock starts on January 1 of the tax year. The number of months late is counted from January 1 of the unpaid year to the current date. Under RA 7160 Section 255, the maximum penalty is capped at 72% (36 months × 2%).

Tax Rates are configurable. The 1% Basic + 1% SEF + 2% monthly penalty are the system defaults. The Administrator can change these per tax year in System Settings → Tax Policy. If the Sangguniang Bayan passes a resolution changing the rate for a specific year, update it there and all new computations will use the new rate automatically.

3.3 Step-by-Step Worked Example

Property with an assessed value of ₱500,000.00, with unpaid taxes for 2023 and 2024, checked in May 2026:

Tax Year 2023 — 29 months late (Jan 2023 to May 2026)
Annual Tax Due = ₱500,000.00 × 0.02 = ₱10,000.00
Penalty = ₱10,000.00 × 0.02 × 29 = ₱5,800.00
Total Due (2023) = ₱10,000.00 + ₱5,800.00 = ₱15,800.00
Tax Year 2024 — 17 months late (Jan 2024 to May 2026)
Annual Tax Due = ₱500,000.00 × 0.02 = ₱10,000.00
Penalty = ₱10,000.00 × 0.02 × 17 = ₱3,400.00
Total Due (2024) = ₱10,000.00 + ₱3,400.00 = ₱13,400.00
Grand Total Delinquency
₱15,800.00 (2023) + ₱13,400.00 (2024) = ₱29,200.00

3.4 How the System Tracks Billing

The system maintains a PropertyBilling record for each property per tax year. This record stores:

  • The assessed value for that year
  • The penalty accumulated
  • Any discount applied
  • The total amount paid against that year
  • The billing status: Pending (nothing paid), Partial (some paid), or Paid (fully settled)

When a property is saved with a tax year, the system automatically creates the billing record for that year. When the Billing Year Sync is run (System Settings), the system creates any missing billing records for all active properties across all years from their effectivity date to the current year.

3.5 Payment Allocation — Oldest Year First

When a payment is posted, the system allocates the amount across unpaid billing years in chronological order — oldest year first. This follows the standard COA practice for delinquent account settlement.

Example — Partial Payment Allocation
Property owes: 2022 (₱12,000) · 2023 (₱15,800) · 2024 (₱13,400)
Cashier posts payment of: ₱20,000.00

Allocation:
  → 2022: ₱12,000.00 fully paid (₱8,000 remaining)
  → 2023: ₱8,000.00 partial payment (₱7,800 still owed)
  → 2024: ₱0.00 (not yet reached)

Result: 2022 is now PAID. 2023 is PARTIAL. 2024 is still PENDING.

3.6 Discounts & Early Payment

The system supports discounts at the billing level. The cashier enters the discount amount when posting the payment. Common discount scenarios under RA 7160:

ScenarioTypical DiscountCondition
Full payment by March 31 (current year)Up to 20%As set by local ordinance
Quarterly installment paid on timeNone (no penalty)Paid within the quarter
Delinquent account — no discount0%Penalty applies instead

The discount is stored against the billing record and deducted from the total due in all computations and reports.

3.7 Configuring Tax Rates

Go to System Settings → Tax Policy. Each tax year has its own rate entry. To change the rate for a specific year:

  1. Select the tax year from the list
  2. Enter the new Basic Rate, SEF Rate, and Penalty Rate
  3. Click Save — the change takes effect immediately for all new computations
Important: Changing a tax rate affects all future computations for that year. It does not retroactively change payments already posted. Always ensure a Sangguniang Bayan resolution authorizes the rate change before updating the system.

4. Quick Reference Card

Cut out and keep at your workstation.

Daily Workflow — Cashier

  1. Log in → Dashboard shows today's collection total
  2. Taxpayer arrives → go to Payment & Receipt Ledger
  3. Search for the property by TDN or owner name
  4. Verify the assessed value and outstanding balance
  5. Enter OR number, date, amount, tax year(s), penalty/discount
  6. Click Post Payment → system confirms and assigns OR
  7. Click Generate Receipt → PDF opens for printing
  8. Hand printed receipt to taxpayer

Daily Workflow — Encoder

  1. Receive new Tax Declaration from Assessor's Office
  2. Go to Property Records → Add New Property
  3. Enter all fields from the Tax Declaration form
  4. Save → system creates billing record automatically
  5. For updated assessments: search existing property → Edit → update Assessed Value → Save

Password & Security Rules

  • Minimum 12 characters — must include uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
  • After 5 failed login attempts, the account is locked for 5 minutes
  • Sessions expire after 1 hour of inactivity — you will be asked to log in again
  • Never share your password. Each staff member must have their own account.
  • If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact the Administrator immediately to reset your password
